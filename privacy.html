Privacy Policy

Why Though? Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Why Though? channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts through their official APIs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API).

It has no public users. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or anyone else. The only data it handles is the access token for the owner's own accounts, kept as an encrypted secret and used only to upload videos and read basic account info needed to post.

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions, TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings, or Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings.

Use of each platform is subject to its own terms and privacy policy: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram.
