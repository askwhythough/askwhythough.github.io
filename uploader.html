Why Though? Uploader

Private tool for the owner of the Why Though? science channel. It publishes the channel's own approved videos to its own TikTok account.

1 · Connect TikTok

Sign in with TikTok and allow the uploader to read your basic profile and post videos you approved.

Continue with TikTok

Permissions asked: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload

2 · Post approved videos

  1. Each finished video is reviewed and approved by the owner first.
  2. At the scheduled time the uploader checks the connected account (name and allowed privacy options), uploads the video file with its caption, and posts it.
  3. The uploader checks the post status until TikTok confirms it is published.
